## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Service** — the hosted Alquist Insight system operated by the Provider,
comprising the Server, the Administration Console and the Client.

1.2 **Server** — the retrieval-augmented generation ("RAG") backend hosted on
infrastructure provided by Hetzner Online GmbH in the European Union.

1.3 **Administration Console** — the authenticated interface through which
Customer content is ingested, managed and monitored. Access is secured by
Keycloak identity and access management.

1.4 **Client** — the anonymous end-user interface for asking questions about
the ingested content. No account or log-in is required.

1.5 **Customer Content** — documents, data and other material supplied by or on
behalf of the Customer and ingested into the Server.

1.6 **Output** — text generated by the Service in response to a question,
produced with the assistance of the third-party large-language-model service of
OpenAI (see clause 5).

1.7 **Session** — a sequence of questions and Outputs associated with a single
randomly generated identifier, used solely to maintain conversational context.
A **new identifier is generated for each new session**; it is not persisted
beyond the session, is not reused, and cannot be linked to any earlier or later
session by the Provider. A Session therefore covers only the exchange that takes
place while the Client remains open in the browser.

architecture, not an operational shortcoming. Session records are deleted in
their entirety on expiry of the retention period set out in
`PRIVACY-NOTICE.md`, or earlier where the storage cap in clause 12.4 is reached.

6.7 **Storage cap.** Stored Session data is additionally subject to a volume limit

# Part C — End User terms (anonymous Client)

## 14. Use of the Client

14.1 You may use the Client to ask questions about the topic the operator has
made available. No account or log-in is required, and we do not ask for your
name or contact details.

14.2 **Please do not enter personal data or confidential information into the
question field** — neither your own nor anyone else's. Questions are stored on
the Server together with a timestamp, as described below.

14.3 A **random identifier** is assigned to your session so that follow-up
questions can be understood in context. It is not derived from your identity, and
a **completely new identifier is created every time you start a new session** —
we have no way of recognizing you as a returning user or of connecting today's
questions to anything you asked before. Your questions, the Outputs and
timestamps are stored and are visible to the operator of the deployment, in
aggregate and individually, for traffic and quality monitoring.

14.4 Answers are generated by an AI system and may be wrong. The Service answers
only within its configured topic and will indicate that it does not know when a
question falls outside it. Verify anything important against the official
source, and contact the operator directly for a binding statement.

14.5 **We cannot delete your data on request, and you should know this before you
type anything.** The identifier for your session is thrown away and regenerated
next time, so once your session ends there is nothing in our records that points
to you. We cannot find "your" questions in order to show them to you, correct
them or erase them, even if you ask us to and even if we want to help. This is
why clause 14.2 matters: **do not enter anything you might later want removed.**
All session records are deleted automatically when the retention period expires.
Article 11(2) GDPR permits us to decline requests under Articles 15 to 20 in
these circumstances, and we will not ask you for identifying information merely
to make such a request possible. See `PRIVACY-NOTICE.md` for the full
explanation.

14.6 If you are a consumer, nothing in these terms affects your mandatory
statutory rights.
